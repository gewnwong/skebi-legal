# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 15, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Skebi** ("the Bot") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you interact with the Bot through Discord.

The Bot is a Discord application designed to create, manage, and deliver scheduled messages within Discord servers.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

The Bot collects and processes information necessary to provide its scheduling functionality.

### 1.1 Discord Identifiers

The Bot may collect and store the following Discord identifiers:

- **User IDs:** Identify users who create or manage scheduled messages.
- **Server IDs (Guild IDs):** Identify the Discord servers where the Bot is installed.
- **Channel IDs:** Identify the Discord channels where scheduled messages should be delivered.
- **Message IDs:** May be stored when necessary to identify or manage messages sent by the Bot.

These identifiers are used to associate schedules with the appropriate users, servers, and channels.

### 1.2 Scheduled Message Information

When users create scheduled messages, the Bot collects and stores information such as:

- Scheduled message content.
- Scheduled delivery date and time.
- Time zone associated with the schedule, when applicable.
- Recurrence settings for recurring schedules.
- Schedule status, such as active, paused, or completed.
- Schedule creation and modification timestamps.
- User ID of the person who created or last modified the schedule.

This information is necessary to create, manage, and deliver scheduled messages.

### 1.3 Discord Interaction Information

When users interact with the Bot through slash commands or other supported Discord interactions, the Bot may process:

- Command names and parameters.
- Interaction IDs.
- User, server, and channel identifiers.
- Permission information necessary to authorize requested actions.
- Relevant Discord API responses.

This information is processed to execute commands, enforce permissions, and provide the Bot's functionality.

### 1.4 Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The Bot is designed to operate through Discord interactions and does not require access to general conversations between server members.

Unless explicitly required by a future feature, the Bot does not intentionally collect or store:

- General conversations between Discord users.
- Direct messages between users.
- Personal contact information such as phone numbers or home addresses.
- Discord account passwords or authentication credentials.
- Payment information.

The Bot does not intentionally monitor or archive ordinary server conversations.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected by the Bot is used for the following purposes:

- Creating and managing scheduled messages.
- Delivering messages to designated Discord channels.
- Processing one-time and recurring schedules.
- Identifying users who create or modify schedules.

- Maintaining and troubleshooting the Bot.
- Preventing abuse, unauthorized access, and misuse.
- Responding to support requests and privacy inquiries.
- Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Information is not intentionally used for advertising, selling personal information, or creating user profiles unrelated to the Bot's functionality.
